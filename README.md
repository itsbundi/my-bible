# My Bible

A personal Bible app: a daily verse chosen for you, reading plans and streaks, highlights, bookmarks, notes, search and listening. It installs on your phone and works offline.

**Open it:** https://itsbundi.github.io/my-bible/

- Android (Chrome): open the link and tap **Install**, or ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**.
- iPhone (Safari): Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

Your name, notes and highlights stay on your phone. Use **You → Backup** to save a copy.

Scripture: Berean Standard Bible (public domain). NLT links open the New Living Translation (© Tyndale House) on Bible Gateway. Fonts: Newsreader and Instrument Sans, SIL Open Font License (see FONT-LICENSES.txt).
